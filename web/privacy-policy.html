Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-10

This Privacy Policy explains how Euro Habesha collects, uses, and protects user data.

1. Data We Collect

  • Account information such as name, email address, and phone number.
  • Profile information and content submitted in the app.
  • Technical and diagnostic information required to operate and secure the service.

2. How We Use Data

  • To provide account access and app functionality.
  • To store and display user profile and service-related content.
  • To improve performance, reliability, and security of the app.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data may be processed by trusted infrastructure providers required to deliver app services.

4. Data Security

User data is transmitted using encrypted channels (HTTPS/TLS). We apply reasonable technical measures to protect data.

5. Data Retention

We retain user data only as long as needed to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

6. Account and Data Deletion

Users can request deletion directly in the app from Profile Settings using the Delete Account option. If app access is unavailable, users can request deletion by contacting support@eurhabesha.com.
