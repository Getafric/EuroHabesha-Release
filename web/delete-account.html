Account and Data Deletion

This page explains how Euro Habesha users can request permanent account deletion and removal of associated personal data.

How to Request Deletion

  1. Open the Euro Habesha app.
  2. Go to Profile Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

If you cannot access the app, send a request by email to support@eurhabesha.com from the same email used in your account.

What Data Is Deleted

  • Account profile data linked to your user ID.
  • User-submitted profile information stored in our database.
  • Associated app token records and account-related metadata where applicable.

Retention and Exceptions

Deletion is processed as soon as possible after confirmation. Some records may be retained only if required for legal, security, or fraud-prevention reasons.
